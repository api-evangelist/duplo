Nigerian church financial management is becoming increasingly complex as churches expand into large, multi-branch organisations with schools, universities, hospitals, media operations, and extensive community infrastructure. The Redeemed Christian Church of God operates over 40,000 parishes across nearly 200 countries. Living Faith Church seats 50,000 worshippers at a single service and runs two universities.
